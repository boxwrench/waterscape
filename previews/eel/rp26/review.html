Visual read. The deterministic before/after pairs use freeze-at-phase-zero captures and normal glint strength. The guard suppresses most of the broad reflected white patches in eye-level and shoreline views; a thin bright angular rim still traces parts of the edge. Useful bank and forest reflections remain. At the bend, authored shallow and reflective contours read more clearly, while the source terrain remains coarse. This is a bounded reflection improvement, not a geometry/contact repair or a production-ready shared change. The Eel-only candidate is ready for human review; no shared adoption is claimed.
Runtime cost | desktop 980 x 820, WebGPU. Both passes report 2,514,254 rendered triangles, 14 draw calls, and 18.03 MiB geometry buffers. RP25's 0.091 MiB optical-depth attribute and the original surface/index buffers are present in both; RP26 adds no triangles, draw calls, or CPU geometry allocation. Eight source textures have an estimated 55.0 MiB RGBA/mip footprint. The 637 x 533 reflection target is estimated at 2.59 MiB color plus 1.30 MiB nominal 32-bit sampled depth. A depth attachment already existed implicitly; sampled depth is newly exposed for RP26's shader, and the incremental/backend allocation is unmeasured.
