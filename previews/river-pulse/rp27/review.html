Eel terrain form. This bounded improvement uses a 1 m source crop; mesh vertices are sampled at 4 m in the primary and phone views, and 2 m in desktop eye-level and shoreline views. The surrounding exported 14 m terrain remains in place. Source elevation fades to the existing triangulated ground over a two-cell seam and is suppressed near refined wet cells to preserve the existing water edge. These are render choices, with no vertical exaggeration. The plain form comparison shows sharper cliffs, benches and gullies, while vertical streaking and soft surrounding slopes remain visible. The Study material softens that gain; olive-toned, smeared near-water surfaces and a bright contact rim remain. This is bounded terrain detail, not reservoir-level scene parity or human acceptance.

Geography and interpretation. Poopenaut terrain remains the USGS 3DEP source grid (14.66 m exported cells, 1x vertical scale); this Tuolumne water pass adds no fine terrain or native-LiDAR claim. The USGS 3DHP centerline supplies the mapped route. The water strip's width, DEM clearance, optical depth, gravel bed and clarity are authored visual controls, not measured width, stage, flow, water depth or bathymetry. The terrain-following reach spans roughly 79 m in elevation, so it uses an analytic sky reflection instead of a single horizontal reflected-scene mirror. Bed refraction, wave normal, and authored shallows remain part of the same water material. Root's visual review finds a clear surface improvement over the prior line guide in all three views; banks and surrounding land remain coarse, with scalloped water contact visible.
Eel runtime cost. In matched Eel phone captures (390 x 844, DPR 1, WebGPU, 12 warmup + 120 samples), previous/native-form passes submitted 2,514,254/2,646,810 triangles and 14/16 calls. Both reported 23.71 MiB resident geometry because comparison buffers remained cached; the decoded 2.29 MiB source crop is excluded. Phone CPU submission was 0.64/0.87 ms previous and 0.65/0.88 ms native (p50/p95); roughly 1004 ms frame intervals indicate browser throttling, not FPS or a speed comparison. The desktop 4 m primary capture reported 2,646,810 triangles, 16 calls, and 0.68/0.81 ms CPU submission, with no matched previous desktop metric. Eight texture maps remain an estimated 55 MiB. Reflection-target estimates are 1.06 MiB color + 0.53 MiB nominal depth on phone and 2.59 + 1.30 MiB on desktop; actual allocation and GPU time are unknown.
Fine Eel tier and Tuolumne phone cost. Desktop Eel eye at 2 m sampling reports 3,079,422 triangles, 16 calls, and 36.42 MiB resident geometry including both cached 2 m and 4 m tiers. Against the different-view 4 m primary capture, that is 432,612 more submitted triangles and 12.71 MiB more resident geometry; it is not an isolated allocation or controlled timing comparison. Water attributes are 0.708 MiB; textures are estimated at 13.33 MiB, with no reflection target/pass. Current CPU submission is 0.87/1.32 ms p50/p95, but previous sampled CPU timing is unavailable.
