Behind this landscape

Geography

USGS 3DEP terrain, approximately 14 m cells, native NAVD88 elevations and no vertical exaggeration. The station anchor and 3DHP river geometry share the local metre frame.

USGS elevation · Mapped river footprint · Scotia station

Visual interpretation

Water and gravel are separated using an authored color classification of aligned USGS/USDA NAIP mosaic imagery inside the mapped river footprint. It is a visual study, not today's wetted channel. The surface follows DEM samples as a proxy; its color and moving highlights are illustrative. No measured stage, current or bathymetry is claimed.

Form mode uses plain terrain and water colors with no animated highlights; the authored water boundary remains. Aerial mode lays the source mosaic on the same terrain. Neither trees nor detailed structures have been built yet.
