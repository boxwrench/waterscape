The close views

The bridge is a detailed, photo-informed reconstruction in its closed position. Its overall length, central span and roadway/deck widths follow the archived 2012 National Bridge Inventory. Heights, member sections, span splits, piers, counterweights, levees and local placement are fitted to photos rather than surveyed.

Bridge angle shows both banks, both road approaches and the underside. Portal signs reproduce the reference photographs; they are not current clearance or travel information.

The marina, moored boats, levee roads, village buildings, fields and vegetation follow the supplied aerial photo as visual context. Their dimensions and placement are authored estimates, not a survey, current boat inventory or seasonal observation. Riverbank opens the marina shore; the overhead view includes the wider riverfront.

The fixed shoreline, green-brown color, clarity and ripples are illustrative. Discharge does not drive water level, bank flooding, wave speed or local current direction.

The terrain view

USGS 3DEP elevation in NAVD88, resampled to approximately 7.7 m cells with no vertical exaggeration. USGS/USDA NAIP aerial imagery follows the same terrain grid; it is a service mosaic, not a current photograph. USGS 3DHP lines share the geographic frame; their display width is symbolic. This is survey geography, not today's water surface or bathymetry.
