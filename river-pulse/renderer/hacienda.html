Read the representation

Terrain preserves source elevations. The colored line shows the mapped river centerline and seasonal flow category—not river width, depth, or water velocity.

Bridge and Hacienda Beach are photo-informed authored scenes with modeled banks, a seven-panel camelback bridge, mixed woodland and shallow gravel water. They are not surveyed banks, infrastructure geometry, bathymetry, gauge stage or measured currents. Map preserves sourced terrain and centerlines. The water ribbon widens with selected discharge relative to the loaded history; its colored border shows seasonal flow condition. Ripples and motion are enlarged for map visibility. Ribbon width is not measured river width or flooding; ripple speed and direction are display choices. Missing or zero discharge stops motion. Beach light, water level and geometry do not change with the timeline.
