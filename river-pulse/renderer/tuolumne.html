Poopenaut terrain and river layout

Native river views use USGS 3DEP at 14.66 m exported cells, absolute NAVD88 metres and 1× vertical scale. The source grid remains unchanged; the authored dam-contact adjustment is described below. These river views have no reservoir water level. The water surface follows the named USGS 3DHP river centerline in a bounded valley reach. Its width, terrain clearance, optical depth, gravel bed and clarity are authored display choices, not measured shoreline, stage, flow or bathymetry. Banks trim against the existing coarse terrain. A study-only contact adjustment lowers 457 coarse terrain vertices by at most 1 m beneath the modeled river corridor. This is a display clearance, not measured channel excavation; Form and Source aerial omit this river-contact adjustment. Shared river optics supply filtered waves, refraction, gravel and highlights. Because the reach slopes across many elevations, it uses an authored sky reflection instead of one horizontal geometry mirror. Review tools retain the previous cyan line guide. Source aerial restores native terrain; Form retains the separate dam-contact treatment while the dam is visible.

Original reservoir visual context

Dam, Below dam and Reservoir views reuse the complete existing Hetch Hetchy renderer and original bundle: granite materials, fine relief shading, terrain lighting, vegetation, water optics and concrete. The reservoir has its own 10.44 m terrain grid and 1151.7 m NAVD88 still-water reference. Its synthetic underwater bed, depth and bank-slope settings, waves, downstream water ribbon and releasing outlet jets are illustrative reservoir content. They are not measured Tuolumne river stage, discharge, bathymetry or a live dam release. The contexts have separate terrain; camera positions alone are translated between their shared UTM 11 / NAVD88 frames. The stock reservoir projection is fixed at approximately 64°, so its comparison with the previous dam study has a different field of view.

The existing dam detail tile is a georeferenced, 1 m-spaced service-resampled normal/relief image. It improves shading; it does not establish native 1 m LiDAR geometry. The native Poopenaut grid is unchanged.

Previous dam study / form comparison

O’Shaughnessy Dam reuses Waterscape’s original crest, gravity-profile geometry and weathered concrete material. UTM zone 11 positions and NAVD88 crest height are translated into the river frame at 1× scale. The crest was hand-traced from NAIP, its elevation sampled from 3DEP, and height/length reported by the USACE National Inventory of Dams. The cross-section is generic, not as-built. Outlet placements are photo-fitted estimates; dark face stains are an authored material effect. The previous study/form presentations contain no water surface or jets. While the 3D dam is visible, a bounded authored terrain cutout clears the DEM smear beneath its face. The source dataset remains unchanged; source-aerial mode and the dam-off baseline restore native terrain. This cutout is a display accommodation, not measured excavation.
