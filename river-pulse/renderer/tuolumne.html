Reused Hetch Hetchy dam

O’Shaughnessy Dam reuses Waterscape’s original crest, gravity-profile geometry and weathered concrete material. UTM zone 11 positions and NAVD88 crest height are translated into this river frame at 1× scale. The crest was hand-traced from NAIP, its elevation sampled from 3DEP, and height/length reported by the USACE National Inventory of Dams. The cross-section is generic, not as-built. Outlet placements are photo-fitted estimates; dark face stains are an authored material effect. No releasing jets, reservoir water level or synthetic bathymetry are imported. While the 3D dam is visible, a bounded authored terrain cutout clears the DEM smear beneath its face, following the existing reservoir treatment. The source dataset remains unchanged; source-aerial mode and the dam-off baseline restore native terrain. This cutout is a display accommodation, not measured excavation.
