Read the representation

The broad earthfill face, gravel crest, outlet portals, concrete wing walls, grey riprap and oak groups are photo-informed authored Setting. Dimensions, banks and camera routes are display choices, not surveyed infrastructure or bathymetry.

Reflections and refraction are computed from a modeled water surface and gravel bed. Green water, ripples and outlet foam are illustrative. They do not establish today's flow, turbidity, dam operations or flood extent. The water level stays fixed.
